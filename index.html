Research Scientist at Mistral AI

Théo Cachet

I was first introduced to imitation and reinforcement learning at Imperial College in 2018, and quickly fell in love with the craft through countless late nights spent training agents for programming competitions such as Halite and Lux AI.

In these challenges, each game starts with random initial conditions and opponents, which motivated me to work on task-level generalization during my PhD at NAVER LABS Europe and Sorbonne University. I developed novel methods for specifying tasks to agents and showed that better task specifications can greatly improve generalization to new tasks. This research resulted in two first-author ICML papers: one on demonstration-conditioned policies, and another on a retrieval-based alternative to VLAs.

I joined Mistral AI a year ago, and led research on Robostral Navigate, an 8B VLM achieving state-of-the-art performance on R2R and RXR. I worked across the entire stack, spanning VLM/VLA architectures, synthetic data pipelines, evaluation, and deployment on real robots.
